# Teacher Guide — Classroom Ready Monday Morning

**Path:** [[classroom-monday]] · **Time:** ~30 minutes · **Grade:** 9–12 / intro college

## Learning objectives

1. Experience social influence through play, not video
2. Distinguish private belief from public behavior
3. Introduce base rates without formulas

## Session flow

| Min | Stop | Prompt |
|-----|------|--------|
| 5 | [[asch-conformity]] | Predict conformity rate |
| 8 | [[milgram-obedience]] | Discuss ethics frame first |
| 7 | [[pluralistic-ignorance-pool]] | "When did everyone follow nobody?" |
| 5 | [[base-rate-hospital]] | Predict-then-reveal |
| 5 | [[standing-ovation]] | Threshold vocabulary |

## Assessment

One paragraph: *Describe a situation where you followed the crowd despite private doubt.*
