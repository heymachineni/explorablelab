# Principal × Innovations Pareto Principle (326)

## Definition

Principal × Innovations Pareto Principle (326) is a compact cognitive tool for deciding under uncertainty. It is not a law of nature—it is a **lens** that highlights some variables and hides others.

## When to use

- Facing a complex decision where principal × innovations pareto principle (326) clarifies trade-offs
- Communicating strategy to a team without full formal model

## When it fails
