## Definition

Updating × Price Signals Market (155) is a compact cognitive tool for deciding under uncertainty. It is not a law of nature—it is a **lens** that highlights some variables and hides others.

## When to use

- Facing a complex decision where updating × price signals market (155) clarifies trade-offs
- Communicating strategy to a team without full formal model

## When it fails
