# Second Order Effects × Updating (426)

## Definition

Second Order Effects × Updating (426) is a compact cognitive tool for deciding under uncertainty. It is not a law of nature—it is a **lens** that highlights some variables and hides others.

## When to use

- Facing a complex decision where second order effects × updating (426) clarifies trade-offs
- Communicating strategy to a team without full formal model

## When it fails
