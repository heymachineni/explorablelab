## Definition

Price Signals Market × Switching (166) is a compact cognitive tool for deciding under uncertainty. It is not a law of nature—it is a **lens** that highlights some variables and hides others.

## When to use

- Facing a complex decision where price signals market × switching (166) clarifies trade-offs
