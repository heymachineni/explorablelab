# Copenhagen Interpretation

> **One-line essence:** Copenhagen Interpretation is a core framework for reasoning about physics.

## Why this matters

Without grasping copenhagen interpretation, practitioners in physics overfit anecdotes to theory and under-specify the variables that actually drive outcomes.

## Core idea

Copenhagen Interpretation formalizes a relationship that practitioners already gesture at informally: which quantities matter, which feedback loops dominate, and where predictions break.
