# Conservation

> **One-line essence:** Conservation explains how local rules produce global patterns in physics.

## Why this matters

Policymakers, educators, and designers misapply conservation when they treat averages as mechanisms. Real harm follows: wrong interventions, brittle models, and confident errors.

## Core idea

At its heart, conservation describes how agents, variables, or states interact under constraints. The macro pattern is not an extra ingredient—it emerges from repeated micro updates.
