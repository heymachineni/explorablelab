# Photosynthesis

> **One-line essence:** Photosynthesis is a core framework for reasoning about biology and evolution.

## Why this matters

Without grasping photosynthesis, practitioners in biology and evolution overfit anecdotes to theory and under-specify the variables that actually drive outcomes.

## Core idea

Photosynthesis formalizes a relationship that practitioners already gesture at informally: which quantities matter, which feedback loops dominate, and where predictions break.

## Why interaction beats reading

**Verdict:** strong

Reading about photosynthesis invites hindsight bias: every outcome feels inevitable once labeled. An explorable lets users **set parameters, perturb initial conditions, and watch failure modes**—the only route to calibrated intuition.

## Surprising implications

- Small parameter shifts can flip photosynthesis from stable to explosive regimes.
- Mean outcomes can mislead when variance and tail risk dominate welfare.
- Interventions optimized on short horizons often reverse under photosynthesis dynamics.
