# Dunning Kruger

> **One-line essence:** Dunning Kruger is a core framework for reasoning about cognitive science.

## Why this matters

Without grasping dunning kruger, practitioners in cognitive science overfit anecdotes to theory and under-specify the variables that actually drive outcomes.

## Core idea

Dunning Kruger formalizes a relationship that practitioners already gesture at informally: which quantities matter, which feedback loops dominate, and where predictions break.

**Verdict:** strong

Reading about dunning kruger invites hindsight bias: every outcome feels inevitable once labeled. An explorable lets users **set parameters, perturb initial conditions, and watch failure modes**—the only route to calibrated intuition.

- Small parameter shifts can flip dunning kruger from stable to explosive regimes.
- Mean outcomes can mislead when variance and tail risk dominate welfare.
- Interventions optimized on short horizons often reverse under dunning kruger dynamics.
