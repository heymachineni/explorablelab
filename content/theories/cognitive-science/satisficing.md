# Satisficing

> **One-line essence:** Choosing the first acceptable option rather than the global optimum

## Why this matters

Choosing the first acceptable option rather than the global optimum. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.
