> **One-line essence:** Self Serving Bias links mechanism, prediction, and intervention in cognitive science.

## Why this matters

Self Serving Bias sits at the fault line between intuition and evidence in cognitive science; misunderstanding it propagates through textbooks, dashboards, and public debate.
