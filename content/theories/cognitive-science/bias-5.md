# Two Prospect Theory

> **One-line essence:** Two Prospect Theory is a core framework for reasoning about cognitive science.

## Why this matters

Without grasping two prospect theory, practitioners in cognitive science overfit anecdotes to theory and under-specify the variables that actually drive outcomes.

## Core idea

Two Prospect Theory formalizes a relationship that practitioners already gesture at informally: which quantities matter, which feedback loops dominate, and where predictions break.

- Small parameter shifts can flip two prospect theory from stable to explosive regimes.
