# Correlation vs Causation

> **One-line essence:** Association does not prove one variable produced another

## Why this matters

Association does not prove one variable produced another. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.

## Core idea

Association does not prove one variable produced another.

## Mechanism

1. **Trigger or input** — situation presents evidence, incentive, or threat.
2. **Process** — minds, markets, or institutions apply rules, heuristics, or structures.
3. **Output** — beliefs, prices, policies, or norms shift — sometimes stabilising, sometimes amplifying error.
4. **Feedback** — outcomes reshape the next round (see [[feedback-loops]]).

## Implications

- Appearances of rational disagreement often mix evidence with structural bias — map which mechanism is active.
- Interventions that ignore second-order effects ([[second-order-effects]]) frequently fail or backfire.
