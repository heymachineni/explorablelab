# Capacity

> **One-line essence:** Capacity explains how local rules produce global patterns in mathematics and probability.

## Why this matters

Policymakers, educators, and designers misapply capacity when they treat averages as mechanisms. Real harm follows: wrong interventions, brittle models, and confident errors.

## Core idea

At its heart, capacity describes how agents, variables, or states interact under constraints. The macro pattern is not an extra ingredient—it emerges from repeated micro updates.

## Why interaction beats reading

**Verdict:** strong

Reading about capacity invites hindsight bias: every outcome feels inevitable once labeled. An explorable lets users **set parameters, perturb initial conditions, and watch failure modes**—the only route to calibrated intuition.

## Surprising implications

- Small parameter shifts can flip capacity from stable to explosive regimes.
- Mean outcomes can mislead when variance and tail risk dominate welfare.
- Interventions optimized on short horizons often reverse under capacity dynamics.

## Real-world applications

- **Education:** teach capacity with sandbox labs before equations.
- **Policy:** stress-test proposals against dynamic mathematics and probability models, not static snapshots.
- **Design:** expose levers users can actually control; hide only complexity that does not change decisions.
