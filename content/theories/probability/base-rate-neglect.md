# Base Rate Neglect

> **One-line essence:** Ignoring how common an outcome is when interpreting evidence

## Why this matters

Ignoring how common an outcome is when interpreting evidence. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.

## Core idea

Ignoring how common an outcome is when interpreting evidence.

## Mechanism

1. **Trigger or input** — situation presents evidence, incentive, or threat.
2. **Process** — minds, markets, or institutions apply rules, heuristics, or structures.
3. **Output** — beliefs, prices, policies, or norms shift — sometimes stabilising, sometimes amplifying error.
4. **Feedback** — outcomes reshape the next round (see [[feedback-loops]]).
