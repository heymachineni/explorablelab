# Confidence

> **One-line essence:** Confidence is a core framework for reasoning about mathematics and probability.

## Why this matters

Without grasping confidence, practitioners in mathematics and probability overfit anecdotes to theory and under-specify the variables that actually drive outcomes.

## Core idea

Confidence formalizes a relationship that practitioners already gesture at informally: which quantities matter, which feedback loops dominate, and where predictions break.
