# Nash Equilibrium

> **One-line essence:** No player gains by unilaterally changing strategy given others' choices

## Why this matters

No player gains by unilaterally changing strategy given others' choices. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.

## Core idea

No player gains by unilaterally changing strategy given others' choices.

## Mechanism

1. **Trigger or input** — situation presents evidence, incentive, or threat.
2. **Process** — minds, markets, or institutions apply rules, heuristics, or structures.
3. **Output** — beliefs, prices, policies, or norms shift — sometimes stabilising, sometimes amplifying error.
4. **Feedback** — outcomes reshape the next round (see [[feedback-loops]]).
