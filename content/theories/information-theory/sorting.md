# Deep Learning

> **One-line essence:** Deep Learning explains how local rules produce global patterns in computer science.

## Why this matters

Policymakers, educators, and designers misapply deep learning when they treat averages as mechanisms. Real harm follows: wrong interventions, brittle models, and confident errors.

## Core idea

At its heart, deep learning describes how agents, variables, or states interact under constraints. The macro pattern is not an extra ingredient—it emerges from repeated micro updates.

## Why interaction beats reading

**Verdict:** strong

Reading about deep learning invites hindsight bias: every outcome feels inevitable once labeled. An explorable lets users **set parameters, perturb initial conditions, and watch failure modes**—the only route to calibrated intuition.

## Surprising implications

- Small parameter shifts can flip deep learning from stable to explosive regimes.
- Mean outcomes can mislead when variance and tail risk dominate welfare.
- Interventions optimized on short horizons often reverse under deep learning dynamics.
