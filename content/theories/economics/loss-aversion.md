# Loss Aversion

> **One-line essence:** Losses loom larger than equivalent gains — roughly twice as salient in many studies

## Why this matters

Losses loom larger than equivalent gains — roughly twice as salient in many studies. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.
