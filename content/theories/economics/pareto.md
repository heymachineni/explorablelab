> **One-line essence:** Coase Theorem links mechanism, prediction, and intervention in economics.

## Why this matters

Coase Theorem sits at the fault line between intuition and evidence in economics; misunderstanding it propagates through textbooks, dashboards, and public debate.

## Core idea

The theory specifies conditions under which observed regularities hold, and—crucially—conditions under which they fail.
