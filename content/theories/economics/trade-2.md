> **One-line essence:** Moral is a core framework for reasoning about economics.

## Why this matters

Without grasping moral, practitioners in economics overfit anecdotes to theory and under-specify the variables that actually drive outcomes.

## Core idea

Moral formalizes a relationship that practitioners already gesture at informally: which quantities matter, which feedback loops dominate, and where predictions break.
