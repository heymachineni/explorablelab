# Public Choice Theory

> **One-line essence:** Public Choice Theory explains how local rules produce global patterns in economics.

## Why this matters

Policymakers, educators, and designers misapply public choice theory when they treat averages as mechanisms. Real harm follows: wrong interventions, brittle models, and confident errors.

## Core idea

At its heart, public choice theory describes how agents, variables, or states interact under constraints. The macro pattern is not an extra ingredient—it emerges from repeated micro updates.

- Small parameter shifts can flip public choice theory from stable to explosive regimes.
- Mean outcomes can mislead when variance and tail risk dominate welfare.
- Interventions optimized on short horizons often reverse under public choice theory dynamics.
