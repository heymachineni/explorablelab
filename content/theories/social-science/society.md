# Scale Free Networks

> **One-line essence:** Scale Free Networks explains how local rules produce global patterns in social science.

## Why this matters

Policymakers, educators, and designers misapply scale free networks when they treat averages as mechanisms. Real harm follows: wrong interventions, brittle models, and confident errors.

## Core idea

At its heart, scale free networks describes how agents, variables, or states interact under constraints. The macro pattern is not an extra ingredient—it emerges from repeated micro updates.

- Small parameter shifts can flip scale free networks from stable to explosive regimes.
- Mean outcomes can mislead when variance and tail risk dominate welfare.
