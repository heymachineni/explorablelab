# Moral Relativism

> **One-line essence:** Moral truth varies by culture or individual — descriptive vs normative claims

## Why this matters

Moral truth varies by culture or individual — descriptive vs normative claims. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.
