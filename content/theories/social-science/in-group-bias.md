# In-Group Bias

> **One-line essence:** Favouring members of one's own group — cooperation and conflict

## Why this matters

Favouring members of one's own group — cooperation and conflict. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.
