# Virtue Ethics

> **One-line essence:** Character and flourishing matter more than isolated acts or rules

## Why this matters

Character and flourishing matter more than isolated acts or rules. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.
