# Cultural Capital

> **One-line essence:** Knowledge, taste, and credentials that confer social advantage

## Why this matters

Knowledge, taste, and credentials that confer social advantage. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.

## Core idea

Knowledge, taste, and credentials that confer social advantage.

## Mechanism

1. **Trigger or input** — situation presents evidence, incentive, or threat.
2. **Process** — minds, markets, or institutions apply rules, heuristics, or structures.
3. **Output** — beliefs, prices, policies, or norms shift — sometimes stabilising, sometimes amplifying error.
4. **Feedback** — outcomes reshape the next round (see [[feedback-loops]]).
