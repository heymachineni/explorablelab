# Legitimacy and Authority

> **One-line essence:** Power persists when subjects believe rulers have right to rule

## Why this matters

Power persists when subjects believe rulers have right to rule. Understanding this idea helps explain patterns in behaviour, institutions, and public debate that otherwise look like isolated mistakes or malice.

## Core idea

Power persists when subjects believe rulers have right to rule.

## Mechanism

1. **Trigger or input** — situation presents evidence, incentive, or threat.
2. **Process** — minds, markets, or institutions apply rules, heuristics, or structures.
3. **Output** — beliefs, prices, policies, or norms shift — sometimes stabilising, sometimes amplifying error.
4. **Feedback** — outcomes reshape the next round (see [[feedback-loops]]).
